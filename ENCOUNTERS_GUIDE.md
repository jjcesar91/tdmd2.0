# 🎮 Strategic Encounters Guide
## Party Composition: Crusader (F) / Prophet (M) / Ranger (R)

### Party Strengths
- **Crusader**: Tank with Gray HP generation (+1/turn), can protect right lane
- **Prophet**: Information advantage - reveals all enemy cards instantly
- **Ranger**: Flexible damage with Range 2, can hit any lane

### Party Weaknesses
- Prophet has NO attack cards (only support/scry)
- Limited card draw (5 cards/turn shared between 3 heroes)
- Crusader can only tank ONE direction (right)

---

## 🗡️ ENCOUNTER TYPES

### 1. "THE PRESSURE" 
**Enemies**: 2 Skeletons + Dark Assassin (middle)
**Challenge**: Which skeleton is real? The assassin deals 3-4 damage bursts!

**Strategy**:
- **Prophet MUST scry immediately** to identify which enemy is the Assassin
- **Ranger** should focus-fire the Assassin (Range 2 advantage)
- **Crusader** tanks the Assassin lane if it's middle/right
- Kill order: Assassin → Skeletons

**Difficulty**: ⭐⭐ (Medium) - Tests information advantage

---

### 2. "THE WALL"
**Enemies**: Armored Knight (F) + Blood Cultist (R)
**Challenge**: Knight has 6 HP + 3 defense. Cultist buffs him every turn!

**Strategy**:
- **IGNORE THE KNIGHT!** Kill Cultist first!
- **Ranger** MUST target Cultist (it's in rear, Knight is front)
- **Prophet** scries to coordinate burst turns
- **Crusader** stalls Knight with defense cards
- If Cultist survives 2+ turns, Knight becomes unkillable (6+ defense/turn)

**Difficulty**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Hard) - Tests target prioritization

---

### 3. "THE FLANKERS"
**Enemies**: 2 Shadow Wraiths (Left + Right lanes)
**Challenge**: Both deal damage to different lanes. Crusader can only block ONE!

**Strategy**:
- **Prophet** scries to see which Wraith attacks harder
- **Crusader** tanks the more dangerous side (right preferred)
- **Ranger** bursts down the undefended Wraith FAST
- **Race condition**: Kill one before Turn 3 or take massive damage

**Difficulty**: ⭐⭐⭐ (Medium-Hard) - Tests multi-threat management

---

### 4. "THE SHIELD WALL"
**Enemies**: Armored Knight (F) + Void Mage (M)
**Challenge**: BOTH spam 2-3 defense cards. Your damage gets nullified!

**Strategy**:
- **This is a DPS check** - Need 4+ damage cards
- **Prophet** scries to find turns where they DON'T defend
- **Ranger + Crusader** must save attack cards and burst together
- **Alternative**: Use Alchemist's Explosive Flask (unpreventable damage)
- Expect a 4-5 turn fight minimum

**Difficulty**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Hard) - Tests patience and card management

---

### 5. "THE NECROMANCER"
**Enemies**: Necromancer (M) + 2 Skeletons (F/R)
**Challenge**: Necromancer "summons" more cards each turn (simulated by extra enemy cards)

**Strategy**:
- **RUSH THE NECROMANCER!** Ignore skeletons!
- **Ranger** focus-fires Necromancer (4 HP, dies in 2-3 hits)
- **Crusader** tanks skeleton damage
- **Prophet** scries Necromancer's defense to time burst
- If Necromancer lives 3+ turns, enemy card advantage overwhelms you

**Difficulty**: ⭐⭐⭐ (Medium-Hard) - Tests target priority under pressure

---

### 6. "THE EXECUTIONER"
**Enemies**: Berserker (M) - SOLO BOSS
**Challenge**: Deals 4-6 damage PER TURN. Can one-shot Prophet/Ranger!

**Strategy**:
- **CRUSADER MUST TANK!** Position in middle lane, use Tank Right
- **Prophet** becomes pure support - scry every turn
- **Ranger** unloads all attack cards (5 HP, needs 3+ turns)
- **Critical**: If Crusader dies, it's a wipe
- Defense cards are ESSENTIAL (Berserker hits for 4-6)

**Difficulty**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Very Hard) - DPS race with instant death risk

---

### 7. "THE PLAGUE"
**Enemies**: Plague Doctor (M) + 2 Skeletons
**Challenge**: Plague Doctor's "Plague Spit" (2 damage) feels like it hits multiple lanes

**Strategy**:
- **Prophet** scries to know when Plague Doctor attacks
- **All heroes** should defend on Plague Doctor's attack turn
- **Ranger** kills Plague Doctor first (3 HP, easy kill)
- Skeletons are cleanup
- Don't let Plague Doctor attack 2+ times (4+ damage to everyone)

**Difficulty**: ⭐⭐⭐ (Medium-Hard) - Tests defensive coordination

---

### 8. "THE CULT"
**Enemies**: Orc Warrior (F) + Blood Cultist (M) + Goblin Shaman (R)
**Challenge**: Cultist buffs Warrior, Shaman provides defense. **Synergy Hell!**

**Strategy**:
- **Kill Order CRITICAL**: Cultist → Shaman → Warrior
- **Ranger** kills Cultist (middle, 4 HP)
- **Prophet** scries to avoid wasting damage on defended targets
- **Crusader** stalls Warrior (5 HP but gets buffed if Cultist lives)
- **If you kill in wrong order**: Warrior becomes 5 HP + 3 defense/turn = impossible

**Difficulty**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Very Hard) - Tests perfect execution

---

## 🐉 BOSS ENCOUNTER: "THE ANCIENT DRAGON"
**Enemies**: Ancient Dragon (M, 15 HP) + 2 Void Mages (F/R, 4 HP each)

**Challenge**: Dragon deals 3-5 damage. Void Mages spam defense (2-3) on Dragon!

**Strategy**:
1. **Turn 1-2**: Kill BOTH Void Mages (Ranger focus-fire, Crusader helps)
2. **Turn 3+**: All-in on Dragon
3. **Prophet** scries every turn to coordinate defense
4. **Crusader** tanks Dragon lane (middle)
5. **Expect 6-8 turn fight** - Manage card resources carefully
6. **If Void Mages live**: Dragon becomes unkillable (4-6 defense/turn)

**Difficulty**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (EXTREME) - Final exam

## 💡 General Strategic Tips

### For This Party (Crusader/Prophet/Ranger):

1. **Prophet is your MVP**: ALWAYS use scry on Turn 1
   - Reveals all enemy cards
   - Lets you plan perfect defense/offense
   - Compensates for lack of Prophet attack cards

2. **Ranger is your DPS**: Save Range 2 cards for priority targets
   - Can hit ANY lane (flexibility is key)
   - Focus backline supports (Cultist, Shaman, Necromancer)
   - Don't waste range on skeletons

3. **Crusader is your anchor**: 
   - Position in Front or Middle lane
   - Use Tank Right when necessary
   - Gray HP stacking = free HP each turn
   - Can survive 2-3 hits without healing

4. **Card Economy**:
   - Prophet has 0 attack cards = only 5 attack cards/turn from Crusader+Ranger
   - Must kill enemies in 3-4 turns or run out of damage
   - Defense cards are ESSENTIAL on burst turns

5. **Lane Positioning**:
   - **Crusader**: Front (lane 0) - tanks left threats
   - **Prophet**: Middle (lane 1) - central for scrying
   - **Ranger**: Rear (lane 2) - safe position, range advantage

## 🎯 Win Conditions

### Easy Encounters (Skeletons, Wraiths):
- Kill in 2-3 turns with focused damage
- Minimal defense needed

### Medium Encounters (Warriors, Shamans):
- 3-4 turns, requires scrying
- 1-2 heroes must defend each turn

### Hard Encounters (Knights, Cultists, Berserker):
- 4-5 turns, perfect card management
- Prophet scry EVERY turn
- Correct kill order or auto-lose

### Boss (Dragon):
- 6-8 turns minimum
- Kill adds first (Void Mages)
- All heroes must defend on Dragon attack turns
- 1 mistake = hero death

## 🔥 Advanced Tactics

### "The Prophet Turn"
- Turn 1: Prophet scries (reveals all)
- Turn 2-3: Team coordinates perfect burst
- Turn 4: Prophet scries again for defense planning

### "The Ranger Snipe"
- Identify backline support (Cultist/Shaman)
- Range 2 card = instant kill (3-4 HP targets)
- Eliminates synergy before it starts

### "The Crusader Wall"
- Stack Gray HP Turns 1-2 (becomes 6+ HP)
- Tank middle lane Turn 3+
- Use Tank Right on high-damage turns

### "The Burst Window"
- Prophet scries, sees all enemies defend
- WAIT one turn
- Next turn: all-in with 5 attack cards
- Kills 1-2 enemies before they can react
